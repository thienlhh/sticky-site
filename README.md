# StickySite

> **Save this view. Reapply it later in one click.**

StickySite is a privacy-first Chrome extension that remembers your search filters, form selections, and dashboard views on any webpage—so you never have to re-configure them again.

---

## Why StickySite?

Modern websites, dashboards, and internal tools often reset your filters every time you visit or refresh. Traditional browser bookmarks don't always preserve dynamic dropdowns, toggles, search inputs, or custom date ranges.

**StickySite fixes this:**
- Set up your favorite filters once.
- Save the view with a descriptive name.
- Restore the entire setup anytime in a single click.

---

## How to Use StickySite

### 1. Saving a View
1. Open any webpage and configure your filters, dropdowns, and search controls as desired.
2. Click the **StickySite** icon in your browser toolbar.
3. Review the captured settings:
   - StickySite automatically detects your URL parameters and interactive form controls.
   - You can uncheck any specific parameter or input you don't want to include.
4. Enter a name for the view (e.g., *"Q3 Open Bugs"*, *"In-Stock Laptops Under $1,000"*) and click **Save View**.

### 2. Applying a Saved View
1. Whenever you want to return to a saved setup, click the **StickySite** icon.
2. Click your saved view from the list.
3. StickySite will:
   - Navigate to the correct page (if you aren't already on it).
   - Automatically fill in dropdowns, checkboxes, text fields, and toggles.
   - Show you an immediate, transparent summary of what was restored.

### 3. Managing Your Views
- **Update View:** Adjusted your filters on the page? Click the `•••` menu next to the view and select **Update with current settings**.
- **Rename View:** Easily rename views to keep them organized.
- **Delete View:** Remove views you no longer need.
- **Clear Site Data:** Remove all views associated with the current website at once from the **Privacy & Settings** screen.

---

## Privacy & Safety by Design

StickySite is built with strict privacy and security guardrails:

- **100% Local Storage:** All your saved views stay entirely on your device in your browser's local storage. There are no user accounts, no cloud servers, and no remote database sync.
- **On-Demand Permissions:** StickySite does not ask for permission to read all your browsing activity upon installation. It only requests access to a specific site when you actively choose to save or apply a view on that site.
- **Sensitive Data Excluded:** StickySite automatically identifies and ignores sensitive inputs:
  - Passwords, credit cards, CVVs, and banking details are **never** captured.
  - Personal identification numbers, phone numbers, and authentication tokens are excluded.
- **No Destructive Actions:** StickySite only restores filter values. It will **never** click submit buttons, purchase buttons, delete actions, or trigger payment forms.
- **Honest Feedback:** If a website updates its layout and some inputs can no longer be found, StickySite clearly notifies you whether a restoration was full, partial, or failed.

## Adding StickySite to Chrome

1. Open Google Chrome and enter `chrome://extensions` in the address bar.
2. Enable **Developer mode** using the toggle in the top-right corner.
3. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left corner.
4. Select the extension folder (the `.output/chrome-mv3` folder inside the StickySite package).
5. Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and click the pin icon next to **StickySite** for easy access.
